# Which pitstop changes from the 13/11 review do we approve?

Decided: 2026-11-13 · Case: c_656f82e5-6747-4463-ae24-0ffb8d5c5b2c

## Question and choice
Review of 61 cycles (56 on 0.5.1). Four changes proposed; all approved:
1. R-MARK: mark-pending warns when results came after the last reply and the checkpoint says SENT.
2. R-LANG: the resume message keeps the language of the chat and of Waggle.
3. R-CMD: "Only the commands that the Next action and the remaining steps run again go word for word, with all arguments." (narrow version)
4. R-GAP: pitstop counts a gap by itself when the resumed session reads the old transcript.

## Why
- R-P3 works when the checkpoint says NOT SENT (3/3); the failure moved to the marking (3 of 6 owed replies, 1 with real harm).
- 6 cycles drifted from Italian to English after the resume.
- 6 cycles searched the old transcript for a command; the narrow rule stops that at low checkpoint cost.
- The report showed 0 gaps while about 10 cycles recovered something from the transcript.

## Constraints
- Exact edits in `~/.claude/pitstop/reviews/20261113-103042/review.md`; ship in release 0.5.2.
- No change may slow the normal flow: R-MARK stops only when results came after the last reply; R-GAP is a text match in the existing hook.

## Ruled out
- R-CMD for all commands in every checkpoint: about the same cost as the searches it saves (estimate).

## Comments
Matteo asked whether R-CMD saves more by keeping or by searching, and whether the changes slow the flow. Answer: small gain either way, narrow rule best; the others add no step in the normal case.
